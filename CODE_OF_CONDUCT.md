# Code of conduct

RVLC adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code
of conduct.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and accept
constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others'
private information and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and
any official communication channel — and when representing the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the contact
listed in the repository's security policy or profile. All reports are reviewed
promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits
or contributions, and may ban contributors who violate this code of conduct,
following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
